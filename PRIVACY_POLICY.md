# Roadio Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Roadio is an Android voice intercom for riders on the same Wi-Fi network or phone hotspot. You can host or join a ride without creating an account.

## Who can hear you

During an active ride, Roadio uses your microphone to detect speech and send live audio to connected riders. This can continue when you switch apps or lock your phone. Mute stops Roadio from sending your voice. Leave Ride ends your connection. Roadio processes audio in temporary buffers and does not save conversations or send them to a Roadio server.

## What nearby devices can see

Roadio exchanges your rider name, a generated rider identifier, room identifier, local network address, and ride status so devices can find each other and show who is connected or speaking. Discovery messages can reach other devices on the same network. A ride QR code contains the room identifier and may include the host’s local network address.

Roadio does not encrypt ride traffic at the app level. Someone with access to network traffic may be able to read ride details or listen to audio. Use a network or password-protected hotspot you trust and share your invite only with intended riders.

## Device permissions

Roadio uses the camera to read ride invites on your device. It does not save or upload camera images. Android may ask for nearby-device or location permission for Wi-Fi discovery. Roadio does not use GPS to track your route. Bluetooth access lets Roadio use compatible earbuds, and an ongoing notification lets you know when the ride audio service is active. You can manage these permissions in Android Settings.

## Storage and deletion

Roadio saves your rider name on your device so you can use it on your next ride. You can change it in the app or remove the saved copy by clearing Roadio’s storage in Android Settings. Roadio does not keep a server-side ride history. Technical diagnostics on the device may include room identifiers and local network addresses. Retention of device logs is controlled by Android.

## External services and purchases

Roadio uses Google Fonts and may contact Google to download a missing font. Google receives the IP address used for that request. Google Play processes app purchases. Roadio does not receive your card or bank details. Google’s handling of information is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Roadio does not sell rider information or use ride audio for advertising.
